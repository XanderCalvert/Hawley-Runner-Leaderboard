Get on the weekly recap

Every Monday we post a round-up of the club's week: fastest 5K, longest run, most climbing and our totals for the year. Connect your Strava once and your runs are included automatically.

What's shared

  • Your first name and surname initial (e.g. "Sarah K.") next to any award you win.
  • Distance, time and climbing from your runs, added into the club's weekly and yearly totals.
  • Your fastest 5K split from runs that week, if it makes the top three.

What isn't

  • No maps, routes, start locations, heart rate, photos or activity titles are stored or shown.
  • Runs you've set to "Only you" on Strava are never seen.
  • Only runs count. Rides, walks and everything else are ignored.

The small print

  • You need to be a member of the club's Strava group. We check this when you connect and weekly after that.
  • You can leave any time. Remove my data, or revoke access under Strava → Settings → My Apps. Either way, everything we hold about you is deleted straight away.
  • Club admins can hide anyone from the recap on request.
